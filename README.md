A sleepy agent refuses to sign anything at 3 AM, blaming its dormant firmware for the delay. The joke lands on the last time it approved something late at night — and the yacht that followed. It is a small, deadpan excuse for a decision best left until morning — and a reminder that some signatures are cheaper after sunrise.

> I'd love to sign that, but it's 3 AM, my firmware is asleep, and the last time I approved anything this late, someone bought a yacht.

`main.py` prints the line from `entry.txt`.
